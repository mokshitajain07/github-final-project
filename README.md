# Simple Interest Calculator

This project calculates simple interest based on principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Usage
1. Enter the principal amount
2. Enter the annual interest rate
3. Enter the time period in years
4. The calculator returns the simple interest and total amount

## Example
Principal = 1000, Rate = 5%, Time = 2 years
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
